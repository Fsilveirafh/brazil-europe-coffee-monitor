# Brazil to Europe Coffee Monitor

## Business question
Where is Brazilian coffee going in Europe, and what do price and exchange rate tell a European buyer: buy now, lock the price, or wait?

## Data sources
| Source | Series | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Comex Stat (MDIC) | Brazilian green coffee exports by destination (NCM 0901.11.10) | Monthly |
| CEPEA/ESALQ | Arabica coffee indicator, R$ per 60 kg bag | Daily |
| Central Bank of Brazil | EUR/BRL and USD/BRL exchange rates | Daily |
